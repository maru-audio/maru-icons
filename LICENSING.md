# Licensing and rights

Maru Icons is a mixed-license collection, not a blanket MIT collection.
Original Maru work uses custom terms that permit use, redistribution and resale.
Contacting us is encouraged, never required. Imported artwork retains its
upstream licenses and brand marks retain their trademark protections.

| Material                             | How to identify it                                                                | Applicable terms                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Original Maru icons                  | Catalog entry without `source` or `brandAsset`                                    | [Maru Icons License 1.1](LICENSE)                                        |
| Maru-authored code and documentation | Package implementations and documentation, except identified third-party material | [Maru Icons License 1.1](LICENSE)                                        |
| Imported artwork                     | Catalog entry with `source.library: "lucide"`                                     | [Lucide ISC and applicable Feather MIT notices](licenses/lucide-LICENSE) |
| Maru logo and Maru Wave              | Entry with `brandAsset`; `MaruLogoIcon` and `MaruWaveIcon`                        | Maru Icons License plus [trademark guidance](TRADEMARKS.md)              |

`licenses/ICON-LICENSES.json` is generated from the canonical catalog and maps
every icon ID/component to its license files. Source details retain upstream
provenance. SVG downloads carry the applicable notices; brand downloads also
carry the trademark notice.

## Use and resale

You may use original Maru icons in personal and commercial apps, websites,
audio plugins, icon packs, UI kits, component libraries, template kits and
frameworks. Modification, redistribution and resale are allowed, including paid
bundles and subscriptions, without separate permission or a royalty. Preserve
the applicable copyright and license notices.

Planning to include Maru Icons in a kit or framework, or resell the collection?
Contact Maru Audio—we would love to hear about it and help this collection
benefit the community. Contact and upstream contribution are optional; neither
is a license condition. No response or approval is needed before use or resale.

Lucide/Feather artwork retains its independent ISC/MIT permissions and notices.
The optional invitation does not replace those licenses or grant trademark rights.

Maru retains copyright in its own work. Contributors retain copyright in theirs.
Trademark rights protect brand identity separately; neither the artwork grant
nor possession of a logo SVG grants permission to brand another product as Maru.
The ™ designation asserts rights without claiming registration.

## Contributions and prior versions

New original contributions use the Maru Icons License. Contributors retain
ownership and must have authority to grant those terms. Third-party artwork
requires compatible provenance and notices. Already granted rights in earlier
MIT/ISC copies are not withdrawn by changing the terms of a future distribution.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The publisher must have the rights needed to apply these terms to each original
contribution. The contribution grant is non-exclusive; contributors retain
ownership and Maru does not claim exclusive ownership of their separate work. This policy does not establish exclusivity over imported artwork.
The custom license should receive legal review before
public release; this document does not claim a court has validated them.
